Third-party markets on Hyperliquid A3 · F4

Markets beyond crypto T1 of 3

Independent deployers run perpetual markets on Hyperliquid that track reference prices linked to companies, including some that are not publicly listed. Sairn shows these markets as an independent interface. It does not create, price or operate them. D1 · D6 · E5 · BC-1 · BC-3

These are perpetual contracts that track a reference price set by an independent deployer. They do not make you an owner of any company: you get no ownership, no dividends and no voting rights. A market can be halted, settled or delisted. S0 · G1 · K1 · E5

Third-party markets can have thin liquidity, and their prices can move while the referenced market is closed. A deployer can settle or delist a market at a price you did not choose. H3 · BC-15

These markets are run by independent deployers on Hyperliquid. Each deployer defines its market, sets its reference price, contract terms and limits, and can halt, settle or delist it. Sairn is an independent interface. It is not Hyperliquid Corp., the Hyper Foundation or any market deployer, and it is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the companies these prices reference. Company names are used only to identify what a reference price follows, and they belong to their owners. Trading these contracts gives you no ownership, dividend, voting or other rights in any company. Sairn charges a 0.01% builder fee on perpetual orders other than TWAP orders, and deployers can set their own fees. Trading through Sairn is not available to persons in the United States or other restricted locations listed in the Terms of Use. Not financial advice. E4 · E5 · K4 · C2 · C3 · B1 · BC-12

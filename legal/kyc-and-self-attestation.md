# Gatu — KYC & Self-Attestation Requirements

> **草案（DRAFT）· 未经合格法律顾问审定前禁止对外发布**　·　品牌 Gatu（加途）　·　官网 gatu.ai　·　应用 app.gatu.ai

## 1. Age & Capacity
Users must be at least the age of majority in their jurisdiction and have full right, power,
and authority to enter into the Terms on behalf of themselves or any entity they represent.

## 2. Self-Attestation
By accepting the Terms, connecting a wallet, or using the Interface, the user represents and
warrants that they:
- are **not a Restricted Person** and are not using the Interface on behalf of any Restricted
  Person;
- do not reside in, are not located in, and are not incorporated in any Restricted Territory,
  and are not a citizen of a Restricted Territory;
- are not subject to economic or trade sanctions and are not owned or controlled by any
  sanctioned party;
- conduct activities that are lawful under all applicable laws.

**Order-submission attestation (UI copy):**
> "By submitting this trade, you agree to our Terms of Use and attest that you are not trading
> from a restricted territory."

## 3. Geographic & Identity Verification (Compliance Controls)
Gatu collects and processes **IP Address, geolocation data, and wallet address**, and may use
third-party **KYC / screening / blockchain-analysis service providers**, to:
1. identify whether a user is likely a Restricted or Prohibited Person;
2. verify identity, identifying information, and location to determine eligibility for the
   Services.

## 4. Anti-Circumvention
Users must **not** use VPNs, proxies, Tor, or similar tools to circumvent geo-blocks or
eligibility rules, and must not make false statements or misrepresentations about residency,
citizenship, or compliance.

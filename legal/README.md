# Sairn legal and policy documents

This directory contains the source documents rendered by the Sairn websites. It is an
engineering source of truth for published copy, not legal advice. Changes to eligibility,
restricted persons, governing law, dispute resolution, data handling, or risk language must
receive qualified legal review before production deployment.

## Document authority

The published [Terms of Use](./terms.md) are the controlling contractual document. The
[Privacy Policy](./privacyPolicy.md) and [Risk Disclosure](./riskDisclosure.md) supplement the
Terms and expressly defer to them where applicable, subject to mandatory law.

Do not create separate region, KYC, or self-attestation lists in this directory. Eligibility,
restricted-person, anti-circumvention, and compliance requirements belong in Section 2 of the
Terms so the product has one authoritative rule set. Superseded drafts remain available in Git
history and must not be restored as operational guidance.

# Sairn - Cookie and Browser Storage Policy

**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Sairn Corp. ("Sairn," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and browser storage in the trading interface available at [https://app.sairn.ai](https://app.sairn.ai).

## 1. Essential Storage

Sairn uses cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar browser capabilities where needed to authenticate a session, connect a wallet, protect the Interface, remember user preferences, and operate trading features.

Examples include language and theme preferences, chart settings, market favorites, account-mode preferences, wallet-provider session data, and locally generated Agent credentials used for authorized trading.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Wallet, authentication, RPC, bridge, security, and infrastructure providers may set or read their own cookies or browser-storage records when their services are used. Their processing is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

## 3. Analytics

As of the Last Updated date, the Interface does not integrate a product-analytics SDK or use advertising cookies. Infrastructure providers may process technical access logs needed to deliver, secure, and diagnose the Services.

## 4. Managing Storage

Browser controls can block or clear cookies and storage. Doing so may disconnect wallets, remove preferences or locally stored Agent credentials, interrupt pending activity, and require account reauthorization. Essential storage cannot be disabled through the Interface while retaining all functionality.

## 5. Related Documents

Review the [Privacy Policy](https://app.sairn.ai/privacy) for information about personal data and the [Terms of Use](https://app.sairn.ai/terms) for the conditions governing use of the Interface.

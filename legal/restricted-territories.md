> **草案（DRAFT）· 未经合格法律顾问审定前禁止对外发布**　·　品牌 Gatu（加途）　·　官网 gatu.ai　·　应用 app.gatu.ai

The Gatu Interface is **not available to "Restricted Persons,"** who are strictly prohibited
from accessing or using it.

## Directly Excluded Countries
- United States of America
- Canada
- United Kingdom

## Sanctioned / Export-Controlled Territories ("Restricted Territories")
- Crimea region
- Cuba
- Iran
- North Korea
- Syria
- Myanmar (Burma)
- Donetsk
- Luhansk
- Any other country subject to comprehensive embargoes or similar sanctions imposed by the
  United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union, the U.N., or any similar authority.

## Excluded Persons
- Citizens of Restricted Territories, **regardless of their location**.
- Persons or entities subject to economic or trade sanctions, and any person owned or
  controlled by such persons or entities.
- Residents of, or entities located/incorporated/with a registered office or principal place
  of business in, **any other jurisdiction Gatu designates from time to time on the Interface
  as restricted**.
